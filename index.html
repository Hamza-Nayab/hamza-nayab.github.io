About Me

Malik Mir Hamza Nayab is a computer science researcher and software engineer who graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from COMSATS University Islamabad, Abbottabad Campus (CGPA 3.72/4.0), and was recognized as Salutatorian at Edwardes College Peshawar. His academic background is grounded in core computing fundamentals, spanning advanced Data Structures & Algorithms, Operating Systems, Parallel & Distributed Computing, and Compiler Construction. For his undergraduate thesis, advised by Ms. Ghanwa Batool, he architected an end-to-end multilingual mental-health conversational system combining Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with custom bilingual (English and Roman Urdu) NLP datasets.

Hamza’s research focuses on algorithmic optimization and low-resource multilingual machine learning. As first author, he developed LadderSort, a novel deterministic adaptive sorting algorithm in C++ that dynamically adapts to the minimum number of nondecreasing subsequences ($K = \mathrm{LDS}(A)$), proving algorithmic optimality and achieving up to 1.45× wall-clock speedups over std::sort and TimSort on 100M-element workloads. In NLP, he formulated the Dialect Robustness Index (DRI) to benchmark Arabic Pre-trained Language Models across five regional dialects, conducted extensive 54-run benchmarks on Roman Urdu emotion classification, and published the largest open-access Roman Urdu GoEmotions dataset (54,263 samples) on Zenodo.

In parallel with academic inquiry, Hamza engineers production software. As sole developer, he built and launched MiddelMen (TrustThread), a social-commerce platform leveraging React/Vite, Node.js/Express, and Neon Serverless PostgreSQL with custom SSR crawler-detection and Cloudflare R2 storage. As Interim Lead, Co-Lead, and Management Lead for Google Developer Groups on Campus (GDGoC) at COMSATS Abbottabad, he directed multi-track technical bootcamps, organized DevFest editions, and spearheaded strategic industry collaborations across Pakistan.
